Operating Model

Determine what is justified before building.

AI engagements begin with the operating problem and the evidence available to support a solution. Assessment can stand alone; build and monitoring are scoped when implementation is justified.

A useful AI system is more than a model. It includes the workflow, data flows, integrations, controls, human review, deployment, and the operating measures that determine whether it is still creating value.

Three-Stage Model

Assess. Build & deploy. Monitor & optimize.

I

Assess

Define the operational problem, readiness, data, systems, business case, priorities, and implementation path.

II

Build & Deploy

Design the system, automation, integrations, controls, testing, and rollout around the actual workflow.

III

Monitor & Optimize

Track technical and business performance, investigate drift or failures, and improve where evidence justifies change.
