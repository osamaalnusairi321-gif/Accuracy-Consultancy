Who We Are

A multidisciplinary consultancy built to adapt.

Accuracy Consultancy & Research was founded in 2005, beginning with enterprise systems and management technology. As client needs and operating environments changed, the firm expanded into field research, verification, Third-Party Monitoring, evaluation, strategy, capacity building, program support, and market entry.

Today, our work is organised around two practices: Advisory & Research and AI & Digital Solutions. One applies evidence, field capability, and structured advisory to complex decisions and programmes. The other helps organisations assess, build, automate, and improve digital and AI-enabled operations.

Across more than 100 assignments for over 20 international clients, Accuracy has combined local reach, analytical discipline, and practical implementation support. Our network includes more than 600 field professionals, with services and delivery capability available in Yemen, Saudi Arabia, and the United States.

How We Work

Start with the problem, not the method.

Our engagements are shaped around the decision, operating problem, or outcome the client needs to achieve. The methodology follows from that, whether the work requires field research, strategic advisory, implementation support, automation, or a combination of disciplines.

We define scope carefully, make assumptions explicit, and distinguish evidence from interpretation. Where technology is involved, we assess whether it is justified before building and design the controls, integrations, and human review needed for reliable operation.

The objective is practical: produce work that can be used, implemented, measured, and improved rather than simply delivered.

1

Understand

Clarify the operating context, decision, stakeholders, constraints, and what success needs to look like.

2

Define

Set the scope, required evidence or data, priorities, risks, dependencies, and measures of success.

3

Design

Build the methodology, delivery model, roadmap, or system architecture appropriate to the engagement.

4

Deliver

Execute the work with clear ownership, disciplined review, and practical coordination with the client.

5

Review & Improve

Measure what changed, identify what needs adjustment, and define the next decision or improvement cycle.
